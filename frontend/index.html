Location
What do these tiers mean?
States are grouped using public NBS/BudgIT economic data (Internally Generated Revenue and GDP), not staff opinion:
Tier 1 — highest economic activity (Lagos, FCT, Rivers)
Tier 2 — strong secondary economic hubs (Ogun, Kano, Oyo, Kaduna, Delta, Anambra, Akwa Ibom, Imo, Enugu, Edo)
Tier 3 — moderate economic activity (most other states)
Tier 4 — lowest recorded economic activity on record; these applications are automatically sent for manual review (never auto-declined) so a human always makes the final call on location-sensitive cases.
Note: location correlates with religion/ethnicity in Nigeria — this is why Tier 4 routes to review, never auto-decline.
